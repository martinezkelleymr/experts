## 2. What GES Does

GES is one of the largest **general services contractors (GSCs)** in live events. A GSC is the company a show organizer hires to physically produce a trade show and to sell services to every exhibitor on the floor. GES also runs an experiential agency and several event-tech and services businesses.

### Customer segments
1. **Show organizers / associations**: hire GES as the official contractor for a show
2. **Exhibitors**: buy booth services, freight, electrical, rentals and graphics from GES at the show
3. **Corporate brands**: hire Spiro for custom exhibits and brand experiences
4. **Venues**: GES offers live-event venue services

### Services for organizers and exhibitors (from ges.com)
- Show planning, floor-plan development
- Graphics layout, proofing and production
- Labor management, material handling, logistics and transportation (advance warehouse, freight)
- Electrical services, rigging, inventory and equipment sourcing
- Exhibitor services: online ordering, cost certainty, "GES Plus" packages, show-site texting, label kiosks, exhibitor education
- Experiential strategy, creative and design, sponsorship consulting (with a dedicated sponsorship tool)
- Accommodations and housing (via onPeak)
- Sustainable offerings / ESG program

### The "GES Collective" (operating brands)

| Brand | What it does | How it joined |
|---|---|---|
| **GES Exhibitions** | Core GSC: exhibition and logistics services (NA and EMEA) | Core |
| **Spiro** | Global experiential agency, custom exhibits, the official EAC (exhibitor-appointed contractor) in the GES family | Created 2022 from the Exhibitgroup/Giltspur legacy |
| **onPeak** | Event housing / hotel room-block management | Acquired 2014 (with Travel Planners) |
| **SHOWTECH** | Power, lighting and electrical services in Canada | Part of GES Canada |
| **Visit** | Event registration and data platform (Europe) | Acquired 2014 |
| **2Heads** | Design-driven brand experience agency (London, LA, Montreal), folded into Spiro | Acquired Dec 2025 |
| **CSG Creative** | Association/event marketing and creative agency | Acquired Nov 2026 |

## 3. History and Acquisition Timeline

GES has grown mostly by acquisition for more than 50 years, so M&A is part of how the company has always worked.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1939 | Manncraft (signs, windows, small exhibits) founded in Kansas City |
| 1950s | Adds an Exhibitor Service Division. Expands to Omaha, Miami, Tampa, Atlanta, New Orleans |
| 1969 | **Greyhound Corp. acquires Manncraft**, renamed Greyhound Exhibition Services ("GES") |
| 1979 | Renamed Greyhound Exposition Services |
| 1987 | Electrical division serves organizers and exhibitors company-wide |
| 1992 | HQ moves to Las Vegas. 500+ full-time staff |
| 1993 | **Acquires United Exposition Service Co.** to go nationwide. Renamed GES Exposition Services |
| 1995 | **Acquires Panex Show Services and Stampede Display** to enter Canada (Toronto, Calgary, Edmonton) |
| 2007 | Viad **acquires Melville Exhibition and Event Services** (UK) |
| 2008 | Melville expands to ADNEC (Abu Dhabi). Viad **acquires Becker Group** (experiential marketing) |
| 2010 | Merges Exhibitgroup/Giltspur, Becker Group and GES. Renamed "Global Experience Specialists" |
| 2014 | **Acquires Blitz Communications** (UK AV), **onPeak + Travel Planners** (US housing) and **Visit** (EU registration) |
| 2017 | **Acquires Poken** (NFC event networking tech, Palo Alto) |
| 2018 | Name shortened to GES |
| 2022 | Spiro experiential agency launched |
| Oct 2024 | Viad agrees to sell GES to Truelink Capital for **$535M** |
| Dec 31, 2024 | Sale closes. Viad becomes Pursuit |
| Dec 2025 | **Acquires 2Heads** (first add-on under Truelink) |
| Nov 5, 2026 | **Acquires CSG Creative** from National Trade Productions (second add-on) |

Over time GES has bought companies for four reasons:
1. **Geographic expansion**: national U.S. coverage, then Canada, then UK/Middle East
2. **Service-line adjacency**: electrical, AV, housing, registration
3. **Experiential/agency capability**: Becker, Exhibitgroup, 2Heads, CSG
4. **Event technology**: Visit, Poken

## 4. Ownership and Deal Economics

### Truelink Capital acquisition (2024)
- **Price:** $535M total. Press reports say $510M was paid at closing (subject to cash and working-capital adjustments), with the rest payable later. The exact terms of the remaining ~$25M are not verified here.
- **Implied multiple:** about **5.9x** adjusted EBITDA, based on a ~$90M midpoint EBITDA (Private Equity Professional).
- **GES 2023 Adjusted EBITDA:** **$68.2M** (Viad investor presentation, Oct 2024).
- **GES revenue:** not separately confirmed in this research. Viad's 2023 consolidated revenue less Pursuit's (~$350M) implies GES revenue of **~$0.9B** (estimate, needs verification). GES is high-revenue and low-margin compared with the attractions business Viad kept.
- **Advisors:** Baird (investment banking), Stax (sell-side commercial diligence for GES).
- **Leadership retained:** Derek Linde (CEO), Jeff Quade (President, GES NA Exhibitions), Jason Stead (President, GES EMEA Exhibitions), Jeff Stelmach (Global President, Spiro).
- GES was Truelink's **first exhibition-industry holding**. The rest of its portfolio is sales training, marketing services, tech platforms and manufacturing.

### Truelink Capital profile
- Los Angeles–based private equity firm, "focused on growth and long-term value creation"
- **AUM: more than $4.3B**
- Sectors: **industrials and business services**
- Key person on GES: **Luke Myers**, Co-Founder and Managing Partner

### Truelink's stated M&A intent
> "...we continue to see a **robust pipeline of M&A opportunities** to build on the platform." (Luke Myers, Nov 2026)

> "...advancing our vision for GES and its brands as the **premier end-to-end partner for live experiences worldwide**." (Luke Myers, Dec 2025)

Truelink is running a **buy-and-build (platform + add-on)** strategy with GES as the platform. Two add-ons have closed in about 11 months, and leadership has said publicly that more are coming.

## 5. Recent Add-On Acquisitions

### 2Heads (December 2025)
- **What:** Immersive, design-driven brand experiences for major technology, financial services and consumer brands. Operating for 40+ years.
- **Where:** London (founded), Los Angeles, Montreal
- **Integration:** Folded into **Spiro**
- **Rationale:** Creative depth, geographic reach and digital capabilities across North America and Europe
- **Sell-side advisor:** PwC Corporate Finance (UK)
- **People:** Paul Godwin (MD, 2Heads)

### CSG Creative (November 5, 2026)
- **What:** Strategic marketing and creative agency for associations and events (~25 years). Its work grows attendance, membership and revenue for shows.
- **Seller:** National Trade Productions, Inc. (Chairman Robert Harar)
- **Integration model:** Runs as an **operating company within GES**. It **keeps its brand**, team and clients. Founder/President **Paige Cardwell** stays.
- **Rationale:** Two-way cross-sell. CSG sells marketing to GES organizer clients, and GES sells exhibition services to CSG's association clients. GES can now offer "a single partner from attendee marketing through show execution."

### Patterns in Truelink-era deals
- **Small, founder-led, capability-adding agencies**, not large GSC mergers so far
- Strong focus on **creative, marketing and experiential** capabilities, moving GES further up the value chain beyond labor and freight
- **Brand retention plus team retention.** The acquired founder stays on.
- Cross-sell into GES's large organizer and exhibitor base is the main synergy story

## 6. Competitive Landscape

### Direct competitors (general services contractors)
| Company | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Freeman** (Dallas) | Market leader in North America, family-owned. Also a serial acquirer (Champion, George Fern, Immersa in 2011, Sparks in 2023) |
| **Shepard** (Atlanta) | 100+ years old, ~500–1,000 employees. Partnered with American Tradeshow Services (2025) |
| **Fern Exposition & Event Services** | Full-service GSC (note: not the same as the George Fern business Freeman bought) |
| **Alliance Nationwide Exposition** | Mid-size GSC |
| **Heritage Exposition Services** | Employee-owned GSC with offices nationwide |
| **The Expo Group** | GSC plus AV and registration |
| Regional / independent GSCs | A fragmented long tail and likely **consolidation targets** |

### Adjacent players and industry deals
- **Encore** (ex-PSAV, Blackstone-controlled): event tech/AV. Filed for IPO in 2026 on ~$3.4B revenue and $466M adjusted EBITDA (13.7% margin). Holds exclusive in-venue AV at ~2,200 venues.
- **Emerald** (trade-show organizer): taken private by **Apollo**, closed July 14, 2026.
- **Informa** agreed to acquire **Clarion Events** for ~$2.97B.
- **Clair Global** (live production) made four acquisitions in 2025.
- Event-tech consolidation is ongoing (registration, lead retrieval, event apps) and is often PE-backed.
- Experiential agencies are a crowded, fragmented market of independent shops.

**Takeaway:** The industry is consolidating at every level: organizers, AV, tech and agencies. Well-funded PE buyers compete for assets, so GES needs to be fast and disciplined.

## 7. Likely Acquisition Theses and Target Profiles

These are inferred from GES's history, Truelink's statements and industry gaps. They are hypotheses to test, not confirmed GES plans.

| Thesis | Example target types | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Regional GSC roll-up** | Independent, regional U.S./Canadian general contractors and exhibitor-service companies | Adds shows and organizer contracts, labor and warehouse density. Classic synergy through overlapping facilities |
| **Association marketing and creative** (CSG-style) | Agencies serving associations: attendee acquisition, membership, sponsorship sales | Directly extends the CSG thesis of "attendee marketing through show execution" |
| **Experiential / custom exhibit agencies** (2Heads-style) | Brand-experience studios, exhibit builders, fabrication shops (especially Europe and North America) | Builds out Spiro's global scale |
| **Event technology** | Registration, badging, lead retrieval, exhibitor portals, floor-plan/booth-sales software, event data/analytics, AI tools | Builds on Visit and onPeak. Recurring revenue and higher margins could lift GES's multiple |
| **Adjacent on-site services** | Electrical, rigging, AV, signage/graphics production, furniture rental, freight/logistics | Keeps more revenue per show in-house (like SHOWTECH) |
| **Geographic expansion** | Contractors and agencies in the Middle East, India, continental Europe, APAC | GES already operates in UAE and India. Growth in Gulf exhibition markets |
| **Housing and travel** | Event housing and room-block firms | Natural tuck-ins for onPeak |
| **Sustainability** | Reusable exhibit systems, event-waste/carbon measurement firms | Supports GES's ESG commitments and growing organizer requirements |

### Typical target characteristics (based on recent deals)
- Founder- or family-owned, **lower middle market** (roughly $5–$50M revenue)
- Strong niche reputation with **long-standing client relationships**
- Leadership willing to **stay on** after the sale, with the brand kept as an operating company
- Clear **cross-sell** story into GES's organizer and exhibitor base

## 8. Implications for an M&A Application

What GES (and Truelink's deal team) would likely value in a purpose-built M&A tool:

### Sourcing and pipeline
- **Target universe database**: GSCs, agencies, event-tech vendors and exhibit builders, tagged by thesis, geography, size and ownership type
- **Signals:** founder age and succession risk, PE ownership and hold period, hiring trends, new show wins, leadership changes, news
- **Relationship mapping:** which targets already work on GES shows or with GES organizer clients (a warm-intro path)
- **Name-collision handling:** "GES" is also an unrelated Spanish energy company and appears in other unrelated deal news, so data feeds need entity disambiguation

### Evaluation
- Thesis-fit scoring (see Section 7)
- **Cross-sell / synergy modeler:** overlap between target clients and GES organizer clients and shows. Revenue synergies (marketing and exhibition services cross-sell) and cost synergies (warehouses, labor, freight)
- Quick valuation: comparable multiples (GES itself traded at about 5.9x EBITDA, and agency, tech and GSC multiples differ a lot)
- Customer-concentration and show-contract renewal-risk analysis (GSC contracts are often multi-year with rebid cycles)

### Execution
- Deal-stage pipeline (Kanban): Sourced, Contacted, NDA, IOI/LOI, Diligence, Signed, Closed
- Diligence checklist and data-room tracker, including union labor agreements (critical for GSCs), venue exclusivity contracts, show-contract terms and key-person retention
- Advisor and contact CRM (bankers, brokers, founders)

### Integration (post-close)
- 100-day plan templates for "keep the brand" operating-company integrations
- Cross-sell tracking: did the CSG-type cross-sell actually happen?
- Retention tracking for founders and key staff, plus earn-out milestone tracking
- Portfolio view of all GES Collective brands and their contribution to revenue and EBITDA

### Stakeholders and likely users
- Derek Linde (CEO) and the GES executive team
- GES Corporate Development / Strategy, and Finance
- Truelink Capital deal team (Luke Myers et al.)
- Business-unit presidents (NA Exhibitions, EMEA, Spiro), who are integration owners
